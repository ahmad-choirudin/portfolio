# Web3 Research Sample

A self-directed portfolio sample demonstrating a structured approach to
researching Web3, blockchain, crypto, and DeFi projects.

> This is an independent research sample created for portfolio purposes.
> It does not represent client research, investment advice, or confidential information.

## 1. Research Objective

The objective is to demonstrate how I structure Web3 research by examining:

- Project purpose and use case
- Blockchain and ecosystem
- Product or protocol functionality
- Token utility
- Community and ecosystem activity
- Potential risks and limitations
- Available public information

## 2. Research Question

**What should be reviewed when researching a Web3 project before forming
an initial understanding of the project?**

## 3. Research Framework

A basic Web3 research process can be divided into several areas:

### Project Overview

- What problem does the project aim to solve?
- What product or service does it provide?
- Who are the intended users?

### Technology

- Which blockchain or network does it use?
- What technologies or protocols are involved?
- How does the product function at a high level?

### Token & Ecosystem

- Does the project have a native token?
- What is the token used for?
- How does the token interact with the ecosystem?
- What other projects, users, or protocols are connected to it?

### Team & Development

- Is information about the team publicly available?
- Is there evidence of ongoing development?
- Are technical documents, repositories, or documentation available?

### Community & Adoption

- How active are the project's public communities?
- Is there evidence of real users or ecosystem activity?
- Are partnerships or integrations publicly documented?

### Risks & Limitations

- What technical risks exist?
- Are there centralization concerns?
- Are there security or smart-contract risks?
- Is the project dependent on a small number of entities?
- What information remains unclear or difficult to verify?

- ## 4. Example Research Analysis

### Example Topic: DeFi Lending Protocol

For this portfolio example, I use a generic DeFi lending protocol rather than
presenting research on a specific project.

### Key Questions

**1. What does the protocol do?**

A DeFi lending protocol generally allows users to supply supported digital
assets to a smart-contract-based system and potentially borrow other supported
assets according to the protocol's rules.

**2. How does the ecosystem work?**

The basic interaction can involve:

- Users supplying assets
- Borrowers providing required collateral
- Smart contracts managing lending and borrowing
- Interest rates changing according to protocol mechanisms
- Users interacting through a blockchain wallet

**3. What should be verified?**

Before reaching a conclusion, I would verify information from public sources
such as:

- Official documentation
- Official project website
- Blockchain explorers
- Public GitHub repositories
- Smart-contract information
- Independent security audit reports
- On-chain activity
- Reputable research sources

**4. What risks should be considered?**

Potential areas for further investigation include:

- Smart-contract vulnerabilities
- Oracle or price-feed risks
- Liquidity limitations
- Governance risks
- Centralization of important components
- Economic or token-mechanism risks
- Dependence on external protocols or infrastructure

## 5. Research Approach

My approach is to separate **verified information**, **interpretation**, and
**open questions**.

I avoid treating marketing claims as established facts and prefer to verify
important information through primary documentation and publicly available
on-chain or technical data.
