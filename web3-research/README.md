# Web3 Research & Analysis

## Overview

A self-directed research portfolio focused on analyzing Web3 projects, blockchain ecosystems, DeFi protocols, and crypto-related developments.

## Research Areas

- Web3 and blockchain projects
- DeFi protocols
- Token utility and ecosystem design
- Project documentation
- On-chain and market research
- Community and ecosystem activity
- Risk and opportunity analysis

## Research Approach

When researching a Web3 project, I typically review:

1. Project documentation and official resources
2. Product and ecosystem
3. Token utility and tokenomics
4. Team and project background
5. Community activity
6. Partnerships and ecosystem development
7. Market and on-chain information
8. Potential risks and uncertainties
