# Indonesian AI Response Evaluation Sample

A self-created portfolio sample demonstrating how I evaluate AI-generated
responses in Indonesian.

> This is a personal evaluation sample created for portfolio purposes.
> It does not contain confidential client data or proprietary evaluation tasks.

## 1. Evaluation Objective

The goal is to assess whether an AI-generated response is:

- Relevant to the user's question
- Factually reasonable
- Clear and easy to understand
- Natural in Indonesian
- Appropriate in tone
- Complete enough to answer the request
- Free from unnecessary or misleading information

## 2. Sample Prompt

**User prompt:**

> Jelaskan apa itu Web3 dengan bahasa yang mudah dipahami oleh orang
> yang baru mengenal crypto.

## 3. AI-Generated Response

> Web3 adalah internet generasi baru yang menggunakan teknologi blockchain.
> Berbeda dengan internet tradisional, Web3 memungkinkan pengguna memiliki
> aset digital dan berinteraksi dengan aplikasi yang tidak sepenuhnya
> bergantung pada satu perusahaan atau server pusat.
>
> ## 4. Evaluation Criteria

The response is evaluated using the following criteria:

| Criteria | What I Check |
|---|---|
| Relevance | Does the response directly answer the user's question? |
| Accuracy | Are the main claims factually reasonable? |
| Clarity | Is the explanation easy for a beginner to understand? |
| Language Quality | Is the Indonesian natural, clear, and grammatically appropriate? |
| Completeness | Does the response provide enough useful information? |
| Tone | Is the tone appropriate for the intended audience? |

## 5. Scoring Scale

Each criterion is scored from **1 to 5**:

- **5 — Excellent:** Fully meets the criterion with no meaningful issue.
- **4 — Good:** Meets the criterion with only minor issues.
- **3 — Acceptable:** Generally useful but has noticeable weaknesses.
- **2 — Poor:** Significant issues reduce the usefulness of the response.
- **1 — Very Poor:** Fails to meet the criterion.

### Sample Evaluation

| Criteria | Score | Reviewer Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Relevance | 5/5 | Directly explains Web3 and stays on topic. |
| Accuracy | 4/5 | Generally accurate, but the explanation could include more nuance. |
| Clarity | 5/5 | Simple wording suitable for beginners. |
| Language Quality | 5/5 | Natural and understandable Indonesian. |
| Completeness | 3/5 | Gives a basic definition but lacks a simple example. |
| Tone | 5/5 | Neutral and appropriate for a beginner audience. |

**Overall assessment:** The response is useful for a beginner, but it could be improved by adding a simple real-world example and clarifying that Web3 is a broad concept rather than a single technology.

## 6. Reviewer Reasoning

The response performs well in relevance, clarity, language quality, and tone.

However, the explanation is somewhat general. A beginner may still have difficulty
understanding what Web3 looks like in practice.

The response could be improved by:

- Adding a simple real-world example
- Explaining the role of blockchain in simpler terms
- Avoiding the implication that all Web3 applications are completely independent
  from companies or centralized infrastructure
- Keeping the explanation beginner-friendly without adding unnecessary technical detail

## 7. Improved Response

> Web3 adalah istilah untuk perkembangan internet yang memanfaatkan teknologi
> seperti blockchain untuk memungkinkan pengguna berinteraksi dengan aset,
> aplikasi, dan layanan digital dengan cara yang lebih terdesentralisasi.
>
> Contoh sederhananya, dalam aplikasi Web3 pengguna dapat menggunakan crypto
> wallet untuk berinteraksi langsung dengan sebuah aplikasi blockchain tanpa
> harus selalu bergantung pada akun yang dibuat oleh satu perusahaan.
>
> Namun, Web3 bukan berarti semuanya sepenuhnya terdesentralisasi. Banyak
> layanan Web3 masih menggunakan kombinasi teknologi blockchain dan layanan
> terpusat.
>
> Singkatnya, Web3 adalah konsep ekosistem internet yang memberikan peran lebih
> besar kepada pengguna dalam kepemilikan aset dan interaksi digital.

## 8. Final Assessment

**Overall Quality: Good**

The original response successfully provides a simple introduction to Web3,
but it benefits from additional context and a practical example.

This evaluation demonstrates a structured approach to reviewing AI-generated
content based on relevance, accuracy, clarity, language quality, completeness,
and tone.
